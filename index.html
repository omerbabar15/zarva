Ask on WhatsApp
Is Cash on Delivery available in my city?

Yes. We deliver with Cash on Delivery to every city and town our courier partners reach in Pakistan, which covers nearly every postal address.

Will one size fit me?

It's cut roomy on purpose, with a 48" chest and dropped shoulders, so it fits most people from about 5'2" to 6'2". Shorter wearers get a slouchier, oversized look; taller wearers get a relaxed regular fit.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 5 PM ship the same day. Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi usually take 2 to 3 working days, other cities 3 to 5.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we'll replace it.

Can I open the parcel before paying?

Courier rules don't let riders wait while parcels are opened, but if anything is wrong when you open it, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll fix it.

Will the colours fade?

The colours are separate fabric panels, not a print. Wash it inside out in cold water and dry it in shade and it will keep its colour.
